Click-through drawings on the real screen for a person to follow: an arrow, ring or box with a sign at a point, a region, a window or a control found by its label, and `draw text` for a label with no arrow. Nothing takes focus and clicks pass through to the app underneath. `draw cover` is the opposite: an opaque box that hides a window, part of it or a token, and follows the window. See [arrows & pointers](/aos/drawing/arrows/), [text labels](/aos/drawing/text/) and [covers](/aos/drawing/covers/).
