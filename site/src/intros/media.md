What is playing and the transport controls. `now` prints the current track, artist, app and position, and exits 1 when nothing plays. `play`, `pause`, `toggle`, `next` and `prev` act on whichever app owns the media session. On macOS `now` reads Music and Spotify (the system-wide Now Playing data is closed to other apps since macOS 15.4) and the controls send the keyboard's media keys; on Linux everything goes through `playerctl` (MPRIS); on Windows the controls send media keys and `now` is not available yet.
