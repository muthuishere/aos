Screenshots and screen recordings. `screenshot` captures a monitor, a region or one app window to a file, without bringing the window to the front. The `video` commands record with ffmpeg and render a second cut where the camera follows the action; see [recording a demo](/aos/capture-video/). On macOS the terminal needs Screen Recording permission for all of them. `text` OCRs a screen area and prints its lines in reading order: `--region` (global, or relative to `--monitor`), or a whole monitor. It uses the same OCR as `window text --source=ocr`: Vision on macOS, Windows.Media.Ocr on Windows, tesseract on Linux.
