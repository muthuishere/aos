Connectivity facts: the active interface and its address, the joined Wi-Fi network, and the local IP on its own for scripts. No display needed. `wifi on`/`wifi off` switch the radio, and `wifi join <ssid>` joins a saved or open network; a password is only ever read from stdin with `--password-stdin` (Linux and Windows; macOS refuses new secured networks because its only route puts the password on the command line). `ports` lists listening TCP ports with the pid and process that own them; give a port to ask who holds it (exit 1 when nobody does), and free it with `aos process kill --port=<n>`.
