Connectivity facts: the active interface and its address, the joined Wi-Fi network, and the local IP on its own for scripts. No display needed. `public-ip` asks Cloudflare's trace endpoint (`1.1.1.1/cdn-cgi/trace`, no account) for the address the internet sees. `vpn status|on|off` drives the VPNs the OS itself manages (`scutil --nc` on macOS, NetworkManager on Linux, `rasdial` on Windows); VPN apps that run their own tunnel outside those settings do not show up. `speed` runs a small speed test against Cloudflare (`speed.cloudflare.com`, no account): a burst of empty requests for median ping and jitter, then 100 KB, 1 MB and the rest of a 5 MB download budget, then 2 MB up; `--max-mb` raises the budget.
