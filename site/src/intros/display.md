Attached monitors. `list` prints each monitor with its geometry and which one is primary, active (has the pointer) and focused (has the frontmost window). `active` and `focused` print just the id, for scripts that pass it to `--monitor`. Monitor numbers can change when displays are plugged in or out. `modes` lists the resolutions a monitor supports; `mode` switches to one (the exact size, the highest refresh rate unless `@Hz` is given, and anything else is refused with the list); `mirror on|off` mirrors every monitor to the main one or extends the desktop again. On macOS changes last for the login session; on Linux they go through `xrandr`.
