title: Record a product demo
description: Record the screen with your voice, and get a cut where the camera zooms to each action.
---

**Goal.** A shareable demo video, recorded in one take, where the viewer sees what matters without manual editing.

## Commands

Check the audio devices, then start:

```sh
aos capture video devices
aos capture video start smart --audio=mic --out=~/Movies/demo.mp4
```

Walk through the product. Every `aos` command you or the agent runs is logged as a camera target. Add your own markers and steer the camera when needed:

```sh
aos subtitle show "Create a project" --seconds=4
aos capture video mark "checkout works"
aos capture video focus --app=Chrome --find="Sign in" --zoom=2 --hold=4s
```

Stop and render:

```sh
aos capture video stop
```

```console
raw:    /Users/you/Movies/demo.mp4 (63.1s)
final:  /Users/you/Movies/demo.final.mp4
events: /Users/you/Movies/demo.events.json
```

Share `demo.final.mp4`. Re-render with different settings without recording again:

```sh
aos capture video zoom ~/Movies/demo.mp4 --max-zoom=2 --ease=0.8 --out=demo.cut.mp4
```

## What to tell an agent

> Record a demo of the signup flow with my microphone. Narrate each step with subtitles, then stop and give me the final file.

## Gotchas

- **ffmpeg must be installed.** `aos doctor` checks.
- **macOS needs Screen Recording permission** for the terminal: `aos permission request screen`.
- **System audio on macOS needs a loopback device** such as BlackHole. `--audio=mic` works without one.
- **The default cap is 10 minutes.** Pass `--max=30m` for longer sessions.
- **Agents should ask before recording.** The skill tells them to.
