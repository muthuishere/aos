---
title: Press a button by name
description: Find a button, menu item or link by its label and press it, without screenshots or coordinates.
---

**Goal.** Press "Equals" in Calculator, or "Wi-Fi" in Settings, by the label a person would read, not a pixel position.

## Commands

Find the control first:

```sh
aos find --app=Calculator --text=7
aos find Safari --text="Reload this page" --role=button --json
aos find --title=Settings --text=Wi-Fi --source=ocr --all
```

`find` prints the control's bounds and center. Exit 3 means the window or the text was not found.

Press it in one step:

```sh
aos mouse click --app=Calculator --find=7 --role=button
aos mouse click --app=Calculator --find=Equals
```

With `--find`, the click goes through the accessibility API. No pointer moves and no focus changes.

Calculate 7 × 6 end to end:

```sh
aos mouse click --app=Calculator --find=7
aos mouse click --app=Calculator --find=Multiply
aos mouse click --app=Calculator --find=6
aos mouse click --app=Calculator --find=Equals
```

## What to tell an agent

> In Calculator, press 7, multiply, 6, equals. Use the button names, do not guess coordinates.

## Gotchas

- **Labels are the accessible names.** On macOS Calculator, × is "Multiply" and = is "Equals". `aos find --all` with a broad `--text` lists candidates.
- **`--role` narrows the match.** Use `button`, `menuitem` or `link` when the same text appears in several places.
- **OCR is the fallback.** `--source=ocr` finds text that has no accessibility node, such as text inside a canvas, but a click then needs the pointer.
- **Needs Accessibility permission on macOS.** `aos permission check` tells you.
